# Break OICO

OICO is useful only if researchers can identify where its measurements fail. This page is an invitation to falsify the package, not an endorsement request.

## Questions to test

- Is QAI mathematically redundant with a familiar normalized backlog-change statistic?
- Does the flagship transformation preserve the definitions used in the EOIR source snapshot?
- Is the interpretation of QAI still too strong even after its descriptive boundary is stated?
- Would a different denominator be more defensible for the same observable process?
- Are missing years, coverage changes, or revised source definitions handled incorrectly?
- Does the frozen snapshot create a reproducibility illusion by hiding source-version drift?
- Are any stored data or derived artifacts redistributed under terms that do not permit it?
- Does the package fail on another supported operating system or Python version?
- Are the claimed invariances, bounds, or benchmark baselines false?
- Do the beta and experimental modules invite users to infer more than their evidence supports?

## How to report a problem

1. Run the clean-room protocol in [REPLICATION.md](REPLICATION.md) when the issue concerns reproduction.
2. Include the release, operating system, Python version, command, observed output, and expected output.
3. Explain whether the problem is computational, data-related, mathematical, interpretive, licensing-related, or documentary.
4. Open the [independent reproduction issue form](https://github.com/SilverCoin256/open-institutional-capacity-observatory/issues/new?template=independent-reproduction.yml), or open a regular issue for a non-reproduction criticism.

Failed reproductions and substantive criticism are valuable results. They will be investigated, linked to the affected artifact, and acknowledged when a public contribution is made.
