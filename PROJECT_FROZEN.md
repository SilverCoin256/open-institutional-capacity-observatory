# OICO Project Freeze

Effective with the `v1.1.1` release, the Open Institutional Capacity Observatory is the final frozen research and software artifact.

Active development is closed. No new features, metrics, studies, dashboards, or admissions-oriented optimization are planned. The repository remains open for independent review, reproduction reports, substantive criticism, and documented downstream use.

Future criticism, replies, reproduction attempts, adoption, citations, and other external evidence will be preserved in the private external-review archive outside this repository. No repository, website, citation metadata, evidence ledger, or release tag will change after `v1.1.1` unless Shaurya Gupta explicitly reopens OICO in a future instruction.
