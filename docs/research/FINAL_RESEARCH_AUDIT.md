# OICO Final Research Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-10

This is an internal scientific and ISEF-style calibration. It is not an official ISEF evaluation, submission, award, or admissions prediction.

## A FINAL SCIENTIFIC CONTRIBUTION

The strongest surviving contribution is an auditable research-infrastructure pattern for public administrative data. OICO makes the full path from frozen source snapshot to derived table, metric, figure, benchmark, notebook, checksum, and limitation inspectable in one low-dependency package. That is a real software and open-science contribution, but it is not yet a validated general theory or benchmark standard.

The empirical contribution is narrower: in the authoritative FY2016-FY2024 EOIR snapshot, pending matters increased 4.749-fold and the normalized backlog-change signal was positive in all eight comparable transitions. QAI is a transparent descriptive convention, not claimed as new mathematics.

## B MAIN EMPIRICAL RESULT

Pending matters rose from 826,488 in FY2016 to 3,925,351 in FY2024. For each FY2017-FY2024 transition, the change in pending matters was positive. Dividing that change by current-year recorded completions produced QAI values from 0.678075 to 1.579835, with a mean of 1.095125.

The result means that the reported pending stock expanded relative to recorded completions throughout the authoritative window. It does not establish cause, staffing adequacy, adjudication quality, legal compliance, or institutional intent. FY2025 and FY2026 remain archived but are excluded from the authoritative claim because the later provenance is not independently recoverable and FY2026 is incomplete as of the audit date.

## I WHAT WAS DEMOTED OR KILLED

- “New QAI statistic” was demoted to “descriptive normalized backlog-change signal.”
- “Institutional capacity measurement” was narrowed to “observable workload pressure and measurement infrastructure.”
- The FY2025 and FY2026 rows were excluded from the authoritative flagship inference.
- Causal claims about staffing, governance quality, failure, intent, and legal compliance were rejected.
- Experimental SEDI, Authorization Saturation, and Procedural Capacity modules were prevented from carrying the flagship claim.
- Synthetic and proxy-label benchmarks were prevented from carrying leaderboard or performance claims.
- DOI, PyPI, external adoption, citation, and ISEF status were left unclaimed.
- Further metric and dashboard expansion is frozen pending outside evidence.

## J BIGGEST REMAINING SCIENTIFIC WEAKNESS

The project has not demonstrated construct validity beyond a single aggregate administrative series. It shows a backlog pattern, but not that the pattern measures institutional capacity, oversight saturation, or administrative congestion as a theoretically distinct construct. The next decisive study needs an independently recovered multi-institution panel, a preregistered measurement model, uncertainty estimates, and an external outcome or intervention for validation.

## M FINAL DECISION

**FREEZE - WAIT FOR EXTERNAL VALIDATION**

OICO is technically and communicatively ready for independent scrutiny of a bounded descriptive result. It is not yet externally validated, benchmark-worthy in the strong leaderboard sense, or entitled to broad institutional-capacity claims. The correct next action is evidence collection by independent researchers, not another round of features, branding, or claims.
