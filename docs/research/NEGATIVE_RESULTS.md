# Negative Results And Abandoned Claims

This record preserves actual failures and limitations from the frozen work. It does not invent a null result where no test was run.

## QAI

- QAI did not demonstrate mathematical novelty; it is a normalized stock-change ratio.
- QAI added little directional information beyond percentage backlog growth in the queue panels.
- QAI cannot identify staffing, waiting time, case mix, or causality.

## Cross-institution comparison

- A pooled institutional ranking was rejected because EOIR, USCIS, CFPB, and SEC differ in unit, frequency, source definitions, and outcome semantics.
- Leave-one-institution-out validation was not run because there is no common ground-truth target.

## Other modules

- ASI remains beta because its corpus is small and inter-rater reliability/generalization are not established.
- SEDI remains experimental because its indicator selection and rolling baselines are theory-laden and externally uncalibrated.
- Authorization Saturation and Procedural Capacity remain scenario tools because their parameters are not observed institution-specific quantities.
- Benchmark leaderboard claims were rejected because labels are proxy-derived, synthetic, or too small.

## Source failures

- The inherited retrieval timestamps for several snapshots are unrecoverable.
- EOIR FY2026 is incomplete as of the audit date; later rows are retained but excluded from the authoritative inference.
- Linked ASI source documents are not redistributed; users must review source-specific terms.
