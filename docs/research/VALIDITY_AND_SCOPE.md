# Validity And Scope

## Construct map

| Intended concept | What OICO observes | What can be said | What cannot be said |
|---|---|---|---|
| Administrative congestion | Pending and completed counts | Workload stock changed relative to recorded throughput. | The institution is overloaded for a known cause. |
| Institutional capacity | No direct staffing, skill, budget, or process measure in the flagship | Capacity questions can motivate data collection. | QAI is capacity itself. |
| Oversight saturation | Small document and workload proxies | A transparent exploratory signal can be computed. | Oversight is ceremonial, ineffective, or illegitimate. |
| AI governance | Textual accountability language and scenario assumptions | Governance language can be coded and audited. | AI systems caused the observed workload or governance outcome. |

## Internal validity risks

- The EOIR file is inherited and its retrieval date is not recoverable.
- Fiscal-year boundaries and reporting definitions may change.
- Aggregate counts do not reveal case mix, age, jurisdiction, judge assignment, or disposition quality.
- Pending stock and completions are not a matched cohort, so a ratio is not a wait-time estimator.
- QAI is undefined when completions are zero and is sensitive to denominator choice.
- FY2025 and FY2026 are not used for the authoritative result because the latter is incomplete as of the audit date and later-row provenance is not independently recoverable.

## External validity risks

The EOIR result should not be generalized to other agencies, countries, courts, or organizational forms without a new source audit and comparability assessment. USCIS, CFPB, SEC, and the ASI corpus are modules and pilots, not replication evidence for the EOIR claim.

## Ethical boundaries

OICO should not be used to rank individual judges, caseworkers, applicants, institutions, or demographic groups. It should not support punitive staffing decisions, automated eligibility decisions, or claims about individual responsibility. Any extension involving sensitive documents or human subjects requires a separate ethics and privacy review.
