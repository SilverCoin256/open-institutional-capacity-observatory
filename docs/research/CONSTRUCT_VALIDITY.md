# Construct Validity: Administrative Congestion

## Operational distinctions

| Concept | Observable proxy | OICO status |
|---|---|---|
| High workload | Pending, received, complaint, filing volume | Observable volume only |
| Backlog | Pending stock at a boundary | Observable stock, definition-sensitive |
| Backlog growth | Change in pending stock | Descriptive flow signal |
| Insufficient throughput | Pending growth relative to completions | Plausible diagnostic, not identified capacity |
| Long waiting time | Age, duration, or processing-time distribution | Not available in the flagship |
| Sudden shock | Change-point or externally dated event | Not validated in Study 1 |
| Legacy caseload | Age-stratified pending stock | Not available |
| Policy-driven demand | Inflow linked to policy change | Not identified |
| Staffing constraint | Staff, judge, budget, or service capacity | Not available |

## Construct verdict

OICO measures observable workload pressure and documentation/throughput traces. It does not measure administrative capacity directly. “Congestion” is acceptable only as a cautious descriptive label for a stock-flow pattern; it must not be silently expanded to quality, effort, legality, intent, or institutional failure.

## Validation required for upgrade

A future construct-validation study needs at least one independent measure of waiting time, case age, staffing/capacity, or service outcome; a pre-specified direction; uncertainty; and replication across institutions with comparable definitions.
