# Ultimate Novelty Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-10.

| Claimed contribution | Classification | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| QAI formula | Already standard / incremental normalization | Not novel mathematics; useful only as a documented convention. |
| EOIR backlog pattern | Previously documented empirical fact | OICO reproduces and packages it; does not discover it. |
| Cross-system descriptive comparison | Novel application, limited | Four systems are inspected without invalid pooling; no external validation yet. |
| Construct boundary between workload and capacity | Incremental conceptual clarification | Scientifically valuable as a guardrail, not a new theory. |
| Provenance and reproduction package | Novel research infrastructure combination | Strongest contribution if outsiders reuse it. |
| Benchmark suite | Early infrastructure | Pilot format only; labels and samples do not support a community leaderboard. |
| AI governance measurement | Experimental application | ASI is a beta text-coding corpus, not validated operational accountability. |

## Literature anchors

- EOIR official statistics: [Workload and Adjudication Statistics](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/workload-and-adjudication-statistics).
- Government evaluation: [GAO-17-438](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-17-438).
- Capacity measurement: [Bednar, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psrm.2023.27).
- Burden-capacity measurement: [Bach et al., American Political Science Review](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/bureaucratic-quality-and-the-gap-between-implementation-burden-and-administrative-capacities/D4F1B8007FCA4753D2803F3EFC8A84A9).
- Conceptual warning against broad capacity shorthand: [Williams, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1744137420000478).

## Final verdict

OICO is best understood as a research-infrastructure contribution with a bounded descriptive study. It is not a new capacity index, a causal explanation, or a validated benchmark standard. The next novelty threshold is independent convergent validity, not more modules.
