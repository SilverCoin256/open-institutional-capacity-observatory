# Study 1 Falsification Record

| Claim | Supporting evidence | Alternative explanation | Distinguishing test | Weakening/falsifying evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EOIR workload expanded relative to completions in FY2016-FY2024 | 8 positive net changes and QAI transitions | Definition or coverage changes | Re-extract agency source versions and compare definitions | Reconciled source data remove the sign pattern |
| QAI is not adding a new direction of evidence | High overlap with backlog-growth baseline | Correlation is driven by common numerator | Compare alternative denominators and sign/rank agreement | A justified denominator materially changes substantive classification |
| Signals should not be pooled across institutions | Different units, frequencies, and outcomes; SEC intensity divergence | Better harmonization could exist | Build a pre-specified common outcome panel | Validated common outcomes show comparable semantics |
| CFPB volume is not service quality | Volume and substantive rate association is not identity | Demand may drive both | Add independent waiting-time or complaint-age outcomes | Volume predicts a validated external quality target after adjustment |
| OICO does not yet measure capacity | No staffing, time, age, or outcome ground truth | Public proxies may still be valid | Compare against independent capacity and processing measures | Replicated convergent validity would upgrade the construct |

The study is designed to lose its strongest claims if a clean source re-extraction, external outcome, or independent replication contradicts them.
