# ISEF-Style Calibration

This document is a self-audit against the official Regeneron ISEF rubric. It is not an official submission, judging result, finalist claim, or award claim.

## Official benchmark

The [official science judging criteria](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/grand-award/criteria/) assess a clear question, design and methodology, execution, creativity and potential impact, and poster/interview presentation. The [Software Design category](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/categories-and-subcategories/software-design/) recognizes software and information processes used to demonstrate, analyze, or control a process or solution. OICO fits the software-design boundary only if the research question and validation remain central.

## Scorecard

| Criterion | Max | Score | Evidence needed to improve |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Question | 10 | 7 | Narrow the platform to a preregistered, comparative question. |
| Design and methodology | 15 | 7 | Add independent sources, controls, alternative measures, and a measurement model. |
| Execution | 20 | 12 | Add complete source provenance, uncertainty, external validation, and larger data. |
| Creativity and potential impact | 20 | 9 | Demonstrate a method that outperforms or saves substantial work versus existing practice. |
| Presentation/interview | 35 | 27 | Show independent use, answer source and validity challenges, and demonstrate ownership. |
| **Total** | **100** | **62** | Internal calibration only. |

## Calibration verdict

OICO is below the empirical depth of the strongest current finalists in this comparator sample. It can be compelling as a software-design project only if its question is narrowed, its ownership is demonstrated, and its external validation path produces real evidence. The current release is ready for that test; it should not be described as meeting an official ISEF finalist or winner standard.
