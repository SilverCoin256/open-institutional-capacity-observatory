# Professor Overview

OICO is a one-week teaching module and research infrastructure case for computational social science, public administration, AI governance, and reproducible methods.

## Why Assign It

Students can reproduce a full research-data release, inspect source provenance, critique proxy metrics, and propose extensions without needing private credentials or a heavy software stack.

## What It Teaches

- Public-data measurement under uncertainty.
- Difference between observables and latent institutional capacity.
- Reproducible pipelines, checksums, and validation reports.
- Benchmark design and the danger of proxy labels.
- Ethical communication of institutional scores.

## Best Fit

Graduate methods courses, PhD seminars, research practicum sessions, or upper-level undergraduate courses with a reproducibility component.

## Caution

Do not present OICO v1 as a definitive institutional ranking. Its value is in transparent measurement infrastructure and critique.
