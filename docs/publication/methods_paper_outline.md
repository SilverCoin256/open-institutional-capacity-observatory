# Methods Paper Outline

## Research Question

How can researchers build reproducible public-data infrastructure for measuring institutional capacity and AI-governance accountability?

## Contribution

- A canonical data model.
- Five transparent metrics or models.
- Public-data stable technical release.
- Benchmark tasks with baselines and caveats.
- Teaching and adoption package.

## Validation

- Reproducibility checks.
- Schema validation.
- Checksum verification.
- Baseline replication.
- External reproduction planned before final release.

## Limitations

Small v1 sample, proxy labels, lack of causal identification, source-license hardening still required, and no claimed external adoption yet.
