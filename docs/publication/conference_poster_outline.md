# Conference Poster Outline

## Title

Open Institutional Capacity Observatory: Reproducible Public Data For Measuring Administrative Congestion And AI Governance Accountability

## Panels

1. Motivation: institutions are capacity-limited, but public measurement is fragmented.
2. Data model: queues, indicators, documents, metrics, provenance.
3. V1 datasets: EOIR, USCIS, CFPB, SEC EDGAR, ASI corpus.
4. Metrics: QAI, SEDI, ASI, authorization saturation, procedural capacity.
5. Benchmarks: tasks, baselines, frozen splits, limitations.
6. Reproducibility: checksums, validation, notebooks, release archive.
7. Adoption path: labs, methods courses, policy researchers.
