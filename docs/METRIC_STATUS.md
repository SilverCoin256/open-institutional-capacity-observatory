# Metric Status and Scientific Boundaries

Status is assigned by empirical defensibility, not implementation effort.

| Component | Status | Construct | Inputs | Legitimate interpretation | Non-interpretation | Validation and next trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| QAI | STABLE | Backlog change relative to recorded completions. | Adjacent pending and completion counts. | A comparable descriptive signal of backlog expansion or contraction. | Not staffing capacity, service quality, intent, causality, or legal compliance. | Formula fixtures, boundary tests, EOIR/USCIS source snapshots. Next trigger: source-definition change or external comparison study. |
| ASI | BETA | Specificity of accountability language in documents. | Eight ordinal coding dimensions over a small adjudicated corpus. | A transparent document-coding measure for comparing the released corpus. | Not operational accountability, implementation quality, or institutional behavior. | Input validation and frozen matrix. Next trigger: larger corpus, inter-rater reliability, and blinded recoding. |
| SEDI | EXPERIMENTAL | A theory-led aggregation of observable degradation signals. | Rolling indicator histories and directional assumptions. | A hypothesis-generating index for exploratory analysis. | Not latent institutional health, human effort, intent, or causal impact. | Numerical edge-case tests only. Next trigger: preregistered construct validation and external calibration. |
| Authorization Saturation | EXPERIMENTAL | Queueing scenarios for substantive versus ceremonial review. | Arrival, service, review, and intervention assumptions. | An interpretable simulation of how utilization changes under scenarios. | Not an estimate of a real organization without parameter calibration. | Algebraic bounds and scenario tests. Next trigger: observed queue and service data with validation targets. |
| Procedural Capacity | EXPERIMENTAL | A bounded risk scenario combining volume, capacity, review, and contestability. | User-supplied scenario parameters. | A transparent sensitivity tool for discussing assumptions. | Not a probability of institutional failure or a causal estimator. | Bounds and invalid-input tests. Next trigger: empirically justified weights and outcome labels. |

## Stability policy

Only STABLE components are presented as release-grade empirical indicators. BETA components may be used with their corpus and measurement caveats. EXPERIMENTAL components are retained because they make assumptions inspectable and support teaching or future research, but they are not allowed to carry the flagship claim.

## General failure conditions

All modules can mislead when source definitions change, reporting coverage is incomplete, missing values are silently treated as zeros, or users infer institutional quality from a public trace. The package therefore keeps quality flags, source checksums, assumptions, limitations, and expected misuse next to the outputs.
