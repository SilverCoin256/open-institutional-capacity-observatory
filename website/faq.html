FAQ

Is this a final benchmark?

No. It is a release candidate with benchmark-shaped tasks, frozen splits, and baselines. Independent labels are still needed for a mature leaderboard.

Does it require credentials?

No. V1 reproduces from frozen public-data snapshots.

Can a professor assign it?

Yes, as a reproducibility and measurement critique module.

Can a lab extend it?

Yes. New sources should start with a source-register proposal and validation plan.
