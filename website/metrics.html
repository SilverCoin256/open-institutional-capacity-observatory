Metric Glossary

QAI

Backlog change normalized by completions. Useful for queue pressure; not causal capacity proof.

SEDI

Bounded degradation signal from standardized public indicators. Useful for monitoring; not ground truth.

ASI

Document coding score for accountability specificity. Textual specificity, not enforcement.

Authorization Saturation

Illustrative model of substantive review becoming ceremonial under load.

Procedural Capacity

Risk model for overload moderated by contestability and ownership safeguards.

Full definitions and failure modes are in docs/concepts/metrics.md.
