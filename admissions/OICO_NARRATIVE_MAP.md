# OICO Narrative Map

1. Organizations increasingly use complex technological decision systems.
2. Those systems generate review, contestation, and oversight demands.
3. Formal accountability does not guarantee substantive institutional capacity.
4. Capacity constraints can sometimes leave observable traces in public workload and review data.
5. OICO builds transparent tools to study those traces without overstating what they identify.
6. The broader question connects computation with organizational and institutional design.

## Field connections

- Computer and data science: data pipelines, validation, packaging, and reproducibility.
- Operations research: queue measures and scenario models, with explicit parameter limits.
- Management and organizational behavior: bottlenecks, review capacity, and decision systems.
- Economics and public policy: administrative workload and public-data measurement.
- Sociology: institutions, accountability, and the social meaning of observable records.
- AI governance: textual accountability specificity and the limits of formal oversight.
