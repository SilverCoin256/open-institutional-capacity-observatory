# Nontechnical Summary

Governments publish numbers showing how much work they receive, finish, and leave waiting. I built an open tool that makes those numbers easier to check and reuse. It keeps the original snapshot, explains every transformation, regenerates the chart, and tells the reader what the result cannot prove.

In one example, the number of pending immigration-court matters rose sharply between 2016 and 2024. That is evidence of a growing reported workload relative to recorded completions, not proof that a particular person or institution failed. The project became more scientifically honest when I tested the limits of its own metric and stopped calling a familiar ratio a new discovery.
