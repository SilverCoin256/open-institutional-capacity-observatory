# OICO Intellectual Arc

## Starting intuition

Public institutions leave workload traces, but aggregate statistics can be mistaken for the institutions themselves.

## Build

I assembled a reproducible open package around that problem: frozen data, provenance, transparent metrics, executable analysis, validation, and teaching material.

## Scientific correction

The audit showed that the named QAI formula is not new mathematics and that a single aggregate series cannot establish institutional capacity. I narrowed the claim rather than increasing the rhetoric.

## Current contribution

OICO is an auditable measurement and replication infrastructure for public administrative workload traces, demonstrated with a bounded EOIR descriptive case.

## Next research question

Can independently recovered workload panels, combined with direct capacity or outcome measures, support a validated comparative construct?

## Why this arc matters

The intellectual value is not only the artifact. It is the decision to separate what the data show from what the theory would like them to show.
