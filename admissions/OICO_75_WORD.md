OICO is open research software for auditing public administrative workload traces. Its reproducible EOIR case finds pending matters rose 4.749-fold from FY2016 to FY2024 and that a normalized backlog-change signal stayed positive across eight comparable transitions. The project includes frozen inputs, provenance, checksums, tests, notebooks, figures, and benchmark pilots. It does not claim a new mathematical index, causality, institutional failure, or external adoption; the next milestone is independent validation.
