# OICO Final Fact Sheet

As of 2026-08-10.

## What it is

OICO is open research software for measuring observable administrative workload pressure from reproducible public-data snapshots.

## What exists

- Public GitHub repository and static research website.
- Stable `v1.1.0` release path.
- Python package and CLI with no runtime dependencies.
- Frozen datasets with checksums, schemas, source register, license notes, and transformation log.
- EOIR flagship case, executable notebooks, tests, figures, benchmark pilots, and replication instructions.
- 25 passing tests, 91% overall coverage, five executable notebooks, and clean-room release checks.

## Main result

In the authoritative FY2016-FY2024 frozen EOIR window, pending matters increased from 826,488 to 3,925,351, a 4.749435-fold increase. QAI was positive in all eight comparable transitions and peaked at 1.579835 in FY2024.

## What it does not prove

It does not identify staffing, intent, case quality, legal compliance, institutional failure, or AI impact. QAI is not claimed as new mathematics.

## External status

Independent reproductions, named lab use, professor recommendation, course link, published citation, conference artifact, DOI, and PyPI release: none verified as of the audit date.
