# Additional Information Building Blocks

OICO is a public research-software project, not a claimed publication, award, or externally adopted platform. The repository contains a stable release path, reproducible source snapshots, a CLI, tests, notebooks, figures, and a research audit. The core empirical finding is descriptive: reported EOIR pending workload grew relative to completions in the FY2016-FY2024 frozen window. The project explicitly does not claim staffing failure, institutional intent, legal noncompliance, causal AI effects, a novel mathematical index, or external validation.

The final audit expanded the research layer into a bounded Study 1 across EOIR, USCIS, CFPB, and SEC snapshots. It found that signals can be compared within systems, but different units and outcomes prevent a defensible pooled institutional ranking. The next milestone is an independent reproduction or methodological critique, not another feature release.
